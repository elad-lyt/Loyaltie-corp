Why We Built Loyaltie

Buying local is broken.
We're fixing it.

Everyone wants to buy local — but convenience always wins

People prefer to buy direct from independent makers. But when it comes to actually doing it, big retailers win — not because people prefer them, but because it's just easier. The intent is there. The infrastructure isn't.

Retailers carry limited brands — most independents never make the shelf

Big retail has finite shelf space. The explosion of D2C independent brands — hundreds of thousands — simply can't fit. Most are invisible to most buyers. They exist, but they can't be found.

Independent brands are scattered — and getting found costs a fortune

Every maker fights for discovery individually — Meta ads, campaigns, influencers. No permanent, browsable home. Expensive to find, expensive to be found. Ordering from several brands means multiple carts, multiple shipping fees, extra friction.

You found something great. Coming back is too hard.

No easy reorder, no easy reorder, no single place to return to. People need one place that holds all their favourite independent brands — where reordering is frictionless and the relationship continues.
