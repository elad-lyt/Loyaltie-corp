Why We Built Loyaltie

Buying local is broken.
We're fixing it.

An explosion of brands — nowhere to find them

Hundreds of thousands of independent brands are selling online. Shopify's growth tells the story. But there is no single point of discovery. The best makers have the smallest ad budgets — they lose on visibility, not quality.

Buyers want local — but convenience always wins

People care about quality, story, and buying from real humans. But when it comes to actually doing it, Amazon wins — because it's just easier. The intention is there. The infrastructure isn't.

The market is fragmented — shipping kills the habit

Buying from three independent brands means three checkouts, three shipping fees, three accounts. You want to support makers — you end up paying a tax on every one. The friction is the barrier.

You found something great. You never found it again.

No easy reorder, no subscription, no single place to return to. Great discovery with zero follow-through. The habit never forms — and the maker loses a customer they already earned.

Paid Acquisition — AI-Powered
📦 PRODUCT CATALOG AGENTS

AI agents scan all products, identify top performers, and split them into two tracks: high-budget catalog ads for proven products and automated individual product ads with AI-generated visuals and copy — launched without manual effort.

Differentiation

Not another marketplace.

Platform Their Position Why Loyaltie is Different
Thrive Market Membership-based health & wellness marketplace. Curated, but brand-controlled wholesale model. Not D2C. Loyaltie is fully D2C — buyers buy directly from makers, not via wholesale distribution. Every purchase goes to the real brand behind it.
Etsy One-time handmade and craft purchases. Gifting-first market. No recurring purchase tools. Loyaltie is the only D2C marketplace built for repeat purchases. Subscription plans are native. Regulars, not one-time buyers.
Amazon Cheap. Fast. Faceless. Scale above everything. Seller is invisible. Human commerce. You know the maker. Every product has a face, a story, a location.
Shopify Individual brand stores. The buyer must already know the brand to find it. A discovery marketplace. Buyers find brands they've never heard of. 420+ independent brands in one place.

The only D2C marketplace where buyers discover independent brands — and make them their regulars.
