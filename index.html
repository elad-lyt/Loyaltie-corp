The Problem

The way people discover
and buy local is broken.

🏪

An explosion of independent brands — nowhere to be found

Tens of thousands of independent brands and local makers are trying to sell online — Shopify's growth tells the story. But there is no single point of discovery. Those already selling face brutal CAC, no recurring revenue tools, and no platform built to solve it.

🧡

Buyers care about local — but convenience wins

People want to buy from real humans. They care about quality and story. But they're not limited to what retailers stock wholesale. They want one place to find it all, with the brands they actually love — not what a retailer decided to carry.

📦

The market is fragmented — and shipping is the tax

Buying from multiple independent brands today means multiple carts, multiple accounts, multiple shipping fees. If you love a coffee brand in Portland and a skincare brand in Austin, you pay to ship from both — separately. That friction kills the habit. Loyaltie covers shipping across every brand, every order, from one account.

🤖

Shopping in the AI era is changing

We still want the human connection and the reason to buy — but we expect a simple AI assistant to find and do it for us. Local commerce hasn't caught up. The old shopping experience doesn't fit the way people want to shop today.

The gap between great independent products and the people who want them has never been bigger.
