About the Founder

Elad Natanson

Co-founder & CEO, Loyaltie

Elad Natanson is a serial entrepreneur since 2002. He has seen the consumer internet change through all its phases — and built companies with it, including a gaming company that to this day sells over $100M annually, and Appnext, a leading mobile app discovery platform. In 2020 he sold Appnext to Affle (NSE: AFFLE), a public company in India, which he then co-ran as a public company board member — growing it from a $400M to a $2.5B valuation — before leaving at the end of 2023. Loyaltie is the company he started purely to create impact: bringing human connection back to the way we buy.
